Cricket Rivals: Design Concept & Architecture

Technical specification, gameplay architecture, and design rationale for the v1.0 release.

1. Detailed Core Loop

I. Authentication & Entry: Fast OAuth login leading to a personalized dashboard featuring global news and active friend status.

II. The Lobby: Real-time matchmaking for 12v12 sessions. Captains are designated based on rank to manage team strategy and batting order.

III. Live Gameplay: High-fidelity 12v12 sync. Each player controls a specific field position or batsman role, with actions processed in <50ms latency.

IV. Post-Match & Results: Automated generation of MVP stats, XP distribution, and social sharing hooks for highlight reels.

2. Design Rationale

Typography: Space Grotesk was selected for its geometric precision and tech-centric personality, bridging the gap between a data-heavy sports simulator and a modern arcade game.

Color Palette: Cricket Green (#2bee2b) provides a high-energy, 'electric' contrast against pure white backgrounds, ensuring critical UI elements (CTAs, live scores) are immediately legible under high-pressure gameplay conditions.

3. Technical Architecture

12v12 Sync Engine: Uses a proprietary state-reconciliation algorithm to ensure 24 players remain perfectly synchronized. Match state is handled by a central authoritative server to prevent client-side spoofing.

Bot AI Logic: The 'Play vs Bot' mode utilizes a behavioral heuristic engine. Bots adapt their bowling line and length based on the player's previous 6 deliveries, simulating a realistic 'learning' opponent.

4. Navigation Deep-Dive

The Global Header functions as a persistent anchor on desktop, housing the 'Quick Play' button and user notifications. On mobile devices, this intelligently collapses into a Context-Aware Bottom Bar.

  • Desktop: Horizontal layout with hovering sub-menus for deep stats.
  • Mobile: Five-tab navigation optimized for thumb-reach, featuring a centralized 'Play' button for instant matchmaking access.

5. Future Roadmap

Clans & Guilds: Competitive team structures with private chat and seasonal trophies.
Weaponized Bats: Rare equipment drops with stat modifiers (Power, Sweet Spot, Durability).
Global Tournaments: 128-team knockouts with real-time spectator modes and live commentary integration.
